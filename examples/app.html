Controls

Right-click / double-click canvas: add node
Drag socket → socket: connect
Drag from a linked input: detach the wire
Alt+click a wire / double-click: cut
Drag on empty canvas: box select
Middle drag anywhere or Space+drag: pan
Right-drag on empty canvas: pan · right-click: menu
Alt+drag empty canvas: pan too
Wheel: zoom · H: frame all
Touch: one finger drags · two fingers pinch to zoom
  and drag to pan · press and hold for the menu
M mute · F collapse · Del delete
Ctrl+D duplicate · Alt+drag copy
Ctrl+C/V/X clipboard · Ctrl+Z/Y undo
Resize a node from its bottom-right corner.
